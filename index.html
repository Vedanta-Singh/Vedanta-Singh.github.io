About

Not a list of buzzwords. More a record of the things I have actually spent time building.

My strongest interests sit where electronics meets intelligent machines. I like understanding the complete chain — the board, the sensor, the controller, the motor, the software and finally the behaviour of the system.

UAVs have been the most visible part of that journey so far, but the direction is broader: embedded hardware, PCB design, VLSI and AI/ML for real physical systems.
